Privacy Policy

Am-Kh Chess — a chess game for mobile

Package name: com.amkh.chessgame
Effective date: August 28, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 7, 2026

Am-Kh Chess is a mobile chess game with fully bilingual English and Arabic interfaces. It lets you play against the Stockfish engine across tiered levels, follow Nour's Path — a journey of 12 stages against the "Nour" characters — play two players on one device, play over Bluetooth with a nearby device, and play online with friends, with ratings, chat, voice and video calls, and an in-game smart assistant. It also includes puzzles, post-game review, a cosmetics store, and play groups.

We do not sell your data and do not share it for marketing purposes, and the app contains no ads and no commercial tracking tools. This page explains exactly what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how to delete it.

1 What works with no data at all

You can use a large part of the game with no account and no internet connection. In these modes, no data is sent to our servers whatsoever:

  • Playing the engine — the chess engine runs entirely on your device.
  • Nour's Path (the levels) — progress and stars are stored on your device.
  • Two players on one device — no network, no account.
  • Playing over Bluetooth — the connection is direct between the two devices and does not pass through our servers.

The data described in the next section is collected only when you create an account or use the online features (online play, friends, chat, calls, ratings, the Nour assistant, notifications).

4 The "Nour" smart assistant

Inside a match you can talk to a smart assistant that explains and analyses in your language. To generate the reply, a text description of the match state (the piece positions, the last move, whose turn it is, the evaluation, your in-game display name, and the text of your message to the assistant) is sent to the AI service provider Groq for processing and returning the reply. The same provider is also used to briefly explain puzzles and to phrase the text of some notifications in your language.

  • Your email address, your private messages with friends, and any account data are not sent.
  • This feature works only when you use the assistant; playing the engine does not need it.
  • Processing of these messages is subject to Groq's privacy policy in addition to this one.

6 Calls and chat

  • Calls (voice and video): transmitted directly between the two devices via WebRTC and encrypted in transit. We do not record or store calls. Our server's role is limited to introducing the two parties to each other to start the connection.
  • Chat: text and voice messages are stored on our server until the recipient receives them and both of you can refer back to them. The connection to the server is encrypted (HTTPS/WSS). Messages are not end-to-end encrypted, meaning the server administrator is technically able to access them; we do not read message content except in the case of an abuse report or technical necessity.
  • Privacy settings: from inside the app you can control who can message you, invite you, or see your presence and picture, and you can block any user.

7 Data retention and deleting your account

We retain your account data for as long as your account exists. Rated-game records stay stored to ensure the integrity of the rating system. At any time you can:

  • Edit your display name, picture and privacy settings from inside the app.
  • Request full deletion of your account and data by contacting us (see section 10). Deletion is carried out within 30 days at most, and covers your account data, your messages, your friends list, your preferences and your device's notification token.
  • We may keep an anonymised match record (without your email, name, or any identifier that points to you) for the integrity of the rating system.

8 Data security

  • All communication between the app and the server goes over encrypted channels (HTTPS and WSS).
  • Access to your account is protected by temporary sign-in codes, and passwords — if used — are stored hashed, not as plain text.
  • Access to the database is limited to those who operate the service.
  • No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we make a reasonable effort to protect your data, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9 Children

The game is suitable for a general audience, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. The social features (chat, calls and player matchmaking) require awareness in dealing with strangers, so we recommend parental supervision. If a guardian learns that a child under 13 has created an account, they can contact us and we will delete the account and its data.
